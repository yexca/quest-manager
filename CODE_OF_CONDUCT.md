# Code of Conduct

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews and other
project-managed community spaces. Everyone should be able to participate
without harassment.

- Be respectful of different experience levels, backgrounds and viewpoints.
- Give specific, constructive feedback about the work. Disagreement is welcome;
  personal attacks, threats, discriminatory abuse and sexual harassment are not.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information, device
  data or communications without permission.
- Avoid spam, repeated disruption and unwanted contact. Respect requests to
  stop an interaction.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, lock a conversation, or limit
participation. Responses should reflect the severity and recurrence of the
behavior. A person affected by a moderation action may ask the maintainer for
review, without reposting private or harmful content.

For a public incident, use GitHub's **Report content** controls where available.
If a private discussion with the maintainer is needed and no private channel is
available, request a contact channel without posting details about the incident
or the people involved. This project does not currently advertise a dedicated
conduct-reporting email or response deadline. Immediate threats should be
reported through appropriate local emergency channels.

Technical vulnerability reports follow [Security](SECURITY.md).
